Anjan Tree

Plant Information

Botanical Name: Hardwickia binata (Roxb)

Common Name: Anjan / Anjan Tree

Family: Fabaceae

Description

Anjan is a medium to large deciduous tree with slender branches and green leaves. It is commonly found in dry regions of India.

Medicinal Uses

  1. Traditionally used in herbal preparations, particularly for skin and digestive-related purposes.
  2. Traditionally used for fever and general body ailments.
  3. Traditionally used for digestive disorders.
  4. Some parts of the plant have been used in Ayurvedic and folk medicine.
